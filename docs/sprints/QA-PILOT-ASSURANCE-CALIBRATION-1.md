## Purpose

Measure operational behavior over sustained activity. Focus on measurement, not adding new capability.

## Measurement Areas

| Area | What to Measure |
|------|-----------------|
| False positives | Findings that do not represent actionable issues |
| Stale-state frequency | Evidence or findings that age without resolution |
| Decision queue quality | Owner decisions: acceptance rate, time to decision, clarity |
| Evidence freshness patterns | Age distribution, stale/fresh ratio over time |
| Projection accuracy | Dashboard matches underlying store state |
| Owner interaction patterns | How often Owner engages with queue, findings, dashboard |
