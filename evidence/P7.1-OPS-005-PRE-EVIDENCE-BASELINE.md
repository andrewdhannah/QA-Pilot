1. **Assume** receipts are conformant (based on project maturity)

- **All conformant** → Confidence increases, no action needed
- **Some non-conformant** → Decision required: fix or accept?
- **Systemic issues** → Decision required: remediate or redesign?
- **Schema drift** → Decision required: update schema or update receipts?

The governance evidence transforms the decision from assumption-based to evidence-based.
